IDENTIFICATION:
Deceased: Nayana Pujari, Female, Aged 28 Years
Identified by: Complainant Abhijit Pujari (Husband) and brother Santosh

EXTERNAL INJURIES:
1. Transverse ligature mark across anterior neck, below thyroid cartilage, measuring
   24 cm x 2.5 cm, with subcutaneous tissue ecchymosis and dark parchmentization.
2. Contusion measuring 6 cm x 4 cm over right temporo-parietal region of scalp.
3. Multiple abrasions and defensive excoriations across both forearms and dorsal hands.
4. Extensive blunt force fracture of fronto-parietal cranial vault with depressed fragments.

INTERNAL EXAMINATION:
- Head: Subdural and subarachnoid hemorrhage present over both cerebral hemispheres.
  Base of skull fractured through middle cranial fossa.
- Neck: Extravasation of blood into sternocleidomastoid muscles and subcutaneous tissue.
  Hyoid bone fractured on left greater cornu.
- Respiratory Organs: Larynx, trachea, and bronchi congested with frothy blood-stained mucus.

CAUSE OF DEATH:
"Asphyxia due to ligature strangulation associated with severe blunt force craniocerebral
injuries (Head Trauma). Injuries are ante-mortem and homicidal in nature, sufficient to
cause death in the ordinary course of nature."
