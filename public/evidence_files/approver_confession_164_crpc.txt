Case Reference: Crime Register No. 534/2009 (Yerwada Police Station)
Date of Recording: 20th January 2010
Presiding Magistrate: Smt. S. V. Sahare, JMFC, Pune Court

NAME OF PERSON CONFESSING:
Rajesh Pandurang Chaudhari
Age: 24 Years | Occupation: Security Guard at Synechron Technologies, Kharadi
Status: Accused No. 4 turned Prosecution Approver under Section 306/307 Cr.P.C.

PRELIMINARY CAUTION BY MAGISTRATE:
"I, S. V. Sahare, JMFC Pune, have explained to you that you are not bound to make
a confession and that if you do so, any confession you make may be used as evidence
against you. Have you had 24 hours in judicial custody free from police influence?"
Accused answers: "Yes, Madam. I wish to state the truth voluntarily."

VERBATIM SUBSTANTIVE STATEMENT (EXTRACT):
"I was on duty as a security guard at the main security barrier of Synechron Technologies
on 7th October 2009. Accused Yogesh Raut was driving the contracted employee transport
Qualis cab. Around 20:00 hours, Yogesh asked me if any female employees were leaving
toward Katraj/Hadapsar. I saw Nayana Pujari leaving on foot toward Kharadi bypass.
Yogesh Raut, along with Mahesh Thakur and Vishwas Kadam, drove off in the Qualis cab.
I remained on duty at the security gate until my shift ended at 22:00 hours, as logged
in the Synechron biometric gate register.

At 23:45 hours, Yogesh Raut called me on my mobile phone from Zarewadi area. He sounded
panicked and told me: 'We have killed her and dumped her body in the ravine. Do not tell
anyone anything.' I was terrified and kept quiet until the Crime Branch detained me.
I had no role in the abduction, sexual assault, or murder. I am ready to depose as a
truthful witness for the prosecution against Yogesh Raut, Mahesh Thakur, and Vishwas Kadam."

JUDICIAL ORDER:
Statement recorded in open court, certified voluntary. Tender of pardon granted under
Section 307 Cr.P.C. on condition of making full and true disclosure.
