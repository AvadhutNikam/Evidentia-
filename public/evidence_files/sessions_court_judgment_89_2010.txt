OPERATIVE ORDER & VERDICT (EXTRACT):
1. Accused No. 1 (Yogesh Ashok Raut), Accused No. 2 (Mahesh Balasaheb Thakur),
   and Accused No. 3 (Vishwas Hindurao Kadam) are hereby CONVICTED of offences
   punishable under:
   - Section 302 read with Section 120B of the Indian Penal Code (Murder & Conspiracy)
   - Section 376(2)(g) of the Indian Penal Code (Gang Rape)
   - Section 397 of the Indian Penal Code (Robbery with attempt to cause death)
   - Section 364 of the Indian Penal Code (Kidnapping for murder)
   - Section 201 of the Indian Penal Code (Destruction of evidence)

2. SENTENCE:
   - Accused No. 1 Yogesh Ashok Raut is SENTENCED TO DEATH by hanging by the neck
     until dead, subject to confirmation by the Hon'ble High Court of Bombay.
   - Accused No. 2 Mahesh Thakur and Accused No. 3 Vishwas Kadam are SENTENCED TO
     RIGOROUS IMPRISONMENT FOR LIFE and fine of INR 20,000 each.

3. APPROVER STATUS:
   - Accused No. 4 Rajesh Pandurang Chaudhari, having complied with the conditions
     of pardon granted under Section 307 Cr.P.C. and having rendered full and true
     disclosure corroborating the recovery and electronic records, is ACQUITTED
     and discharged.

Signed and Sealed,
Special Judge L. L. Yenkar, Pune Sessions Court.
