================================================================================
MEMORANDUM OF DISCOVERY & RECOVERY PANCHNAMA
(Under Section 27 of the Indian Evidence Act, 1872)
================================================================================
Case: Sessions Case No. 89 of 2010 (Yerwada PS FIR No. 534/2009)
Date of Panchnama: 17th October 2009 between 11:15 Hours and 14:00 Hours
Place: Crime Branch Unit 4, Pune, and Residence of Accused Yogesh Ashok Raut

INDEPENDENT PANCH WITNESSES:
1. Shrikant Ramchandra Joshi, Age 44, Business, Pune
2. Dilip Shankar Patil, Age 39, Private Service, Pune

STATEMENT OF ACCUSED IN POLICE CUSTODY (Under Sec 27):
Accused No. 1, Yogesh Ashok Raut, in presence of above panchas voluntarily made
the following disclosure statement in Marathi:
"I have concealed the black leather office laptop bag containing the Hewlett-Packard
laptop, gold chain, wristwatch, and ATM card belonging to Nayana Pujari inside the
lofted wooden storage above the kitchen at my rented residence in Hadapsar. I will
point out the location and produce the same."

RECOVERY PROCEEDINGS:
The police team led by Police Inspector Kishor Mhaswade, along with the two panchas
and accused Yogesh Raut, proceeded to Room No. 3, Chawl No. 12, Hadapsar, Pune.
Accused unlocked the door, climbed a stool, and retrieved from the kitchen loft:
1. One black Hewlett-Packard (HP) Laptop bearing Serial No. CNF842XXXX, with
   Synechron company asset barcode tag #SYN-IT-08412.
2. One gold mangalsutra with black beads, weighing approx 14 grams.
3. One ladies wristwatch (Titan Raga) with broken steel mesh strap.
4. One State Bank of India ATM Debit Card in the name of Nayana Pujari.

SEIZURE & SEALING:
All four articles were inspected by the panchas, labeled, packed in paper envelopes,
and sealed with the wax seal of "P.I. CRIME BRANCH PUNE". Signed by both panchas,
accused Yogesh Raut, and PI Kishor Mhaswade.

[Admitted as Prosecution Exhibit No. 18, Sessions Case No. 89/2010]
